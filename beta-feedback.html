Beta tester feedback

Tell us how it's going.

Four short sections, about five minutes. Answer what applies — skip anything you haven't tried. Name and email are optional if you'd rather stay anonymous.

01

Download & Installation

Getting the app onto your phone and open for the first time.

Did you install FilsOnly via the Play Store link without any issues?
Was anything about the Play Store listing confusing or different from what you expected?
Did the app open and load correctly on first launch?
02

Email Setup & Inbox Sync

Connecting Gmail or Outlook and how your inbox showed up.

Did connecting Gmail/Outlook work smoothly?
Once connected, did your inbox show correctly?
Did Open/Closed tabs behave as expected?
Did only Primary/Focused emails show, without Promotions/Social/Updates clutter?
Did tapping an email open it correctly?
03

Features & Functionality

Draft replies, tracking, notifications, and general stability.

Did draft replies feel accurate/relevant?
Did the Track feature work as expected (tag, find, untrack)?
Did local delete work as expected?
Did you get notified about anything waiting for a reply?
With the app closed, did you still get notified about anything urgent?
Did anything crash, freeze, or behave unexpectedly?
04

Overall Feedback

The big picture — how it compares, and what's next.

In one sentence, how would you describe this app to a friend?
Does this feel meaningfully different from your normal Gmail/Outlook app?
Would you keep using this after the beta?
Anything that made you not trust the app?
What's the one thing you'd want fixed or added next?

Goes straight to the FilsOnly team — thank you for testing.
